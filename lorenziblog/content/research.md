### Photonics and electromagnetics

I study light propagation with tools borrowed from quantum mechanics and field theory. My work in this direction includes material and structural slow light, nonlinear effects in waveguides, geometric algebra for classical electrodynamics, and optical solitons.

### Optical communications and simulations

I develop implementation-level numerical models for high-throughput optical communications. I focus in particular on realistic noise estimation in systems where classical channels coexist with QKD channels.
