    <h3>About me</h3>
    <p>I am a <strong>researcher</strong> in the field of <strong>theoretical condensed matter physics</strong> and its applications.</p>
    <p>My current position is postdoctoral researcher in photonics and nonlinear optics, under the supervision of Prof. M. Santagiustina. I did my Ph.D. on the remarkable bridge between the physics of Bose&ndash;Einstein condensates and that of nonlinear waves in optical media, supervised by Prof. L. Salasnich.</p>
    <p>I am mostly interested in describing wave phenomena in photonic and quantum systems. Some of my specific interests include:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Nonlinear wave propagation and integrability / soliton theory</li>
      <li>Theoretical photonics and electromagnetism</li>
      <li>Stochastic modeling for optical communications</li>
      <li>Ultracold quantum gases</li>
    </ul>
